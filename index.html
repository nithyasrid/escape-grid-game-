HOW TO PLAY

ESCAPE THE GRID

🎯 GOAL

Complete the objective shown at the top and reach the green exit.

⌨️ PC

WASD or Arrow Keys = move
R = restart level
H = request a hint

📱 MOBILE

Use the four on-screen direction buttons to move.

💡 HINTS

You get 3 hints per level. A hint costs 50 points. If you stay stuck for 45 seconds, a hint appears automatically.

⚠️ SABOTAGE

Every level has a different sabotage event. Read the sabotage line before moving.

🏆 PROGRESS

Finish levels to unlock the next ones. Your score and progress are saved on this device.
